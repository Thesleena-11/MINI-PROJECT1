# MINI-PROJECT1 
Project focuses on analyzing a retail sales dataset to understand Business performance across categories,customers,salespersons.
      RETAIL PERFORMANCE OVERVIEW
Project Overview

The Retail Performance Overview dashboard is a Power BI analytical report designed to monitor and evaluate overall retail business performance.
It provides insights into sales, profit, customers, products, and salesperson performance across different time periods.

The dashboard helps stakeholders identify:

Revenue trends
Profitability issues
Customer contribution
Top-performing salespersons
Quarterly and yearly business fluctuations

Objectives:
Track overall retail sales and profitability
Analyze customer purchasing behavior
Identify top-performing salespersons
Understand reasons behind profit decline
Monitor quarterly sales trends
Support data-driven business decisions

Visualizations Used
KPI Cards
Line Charts
Bar/Column Charts
Pie/Donut Charts
Tables & Matrix Visuals
Slicers

Key Insights
1. Top Customers
A small group of customers contributed a significant percentage of total revenue.
High-value customers generated repeated purchases, indicating strong customer loyalty.
Focus on retention strategies for premium customers can improve long-term profitability.
2. Top Performing Salesperson
Certain salespersons consistently achieved higher sales and profit margins.
Their performance was driven by:
Higher order volumes
Better customer relationships
Strong regional performance
Best-performing salespersons can be used as benchmarks for sales strategy and training.
3. Reason for Profit Decrease (Yearly)

The report identified several possible reasons behind declining yearly profit:

Increased operational or product costs
Heavy discounting strategies reducing margins
Lower profit contribution from certain product categories
Rising sales returns or shipping costs
Decline in high-margin product sales

Despite stable sales in some periods, profit margins reduced due to higher expenses and lower-margin transactions.
4. Reason for Sales Decrease (Quarterly)

Quarterly sales analysis showed noticeable declines due to:

Seasonal demand fluctuations
Reduced customer orders in specific quarters
Weak performance in selected regions/categories
Lower promotional activity
Inventory or supply chain limitations

The dashboard helps identify which quarters require stronger marketing and sales strategies.

Tools & Technologies:
Power BI Desktop
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
Power Query
Excel/CSV Dataset

Conclusion

The Retail Performance Overview dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of retail operations through interactive visuals and KPIs.
It enables businesses to monitor sales trends, profitability, customer behavior, and employee performance while identifying critical factors affecting business growth.
